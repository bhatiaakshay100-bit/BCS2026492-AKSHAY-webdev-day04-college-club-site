iTech Club Abhiruchi at Invertis University is a student-driven technology community that brings together curious minds, creative thinkers and future innovators. From coding and technology to events and competitions, we believe in learning by doing and growing together.

Who we are

Turning ideas into innovation.

College is not just about classrooms and textbooks. It is about exploring new opportunities, developing skills and working with people who share the same passion. That's what iTech Club Abhiruchi stands for.

We organise technology-focused activities, workshops, competitions, creative events and interactive sessions that encourage students to step beyond their comfort zones. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced learner, there is always something new to explore.
